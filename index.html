About the book

Influence is not an accident.
It is architected.

Every leader eventually meets the moment where titles stop working and influence must take over. The Architecture of Influence draws 24 timeless laws from the lives of Mordecai and Esther — showing how ordinary people in impossible rooms leveraged positioning, wisdom and favour to alter the destiny of a nation.

  • Lead when you have no title — and still matter when you're not in the room.
  • Read the politics of any room without losing your integrity.
  • Positioning, timing and favour: the quiet mechanics behind open doors.
  • Build a legacy that outlives your position.
